Sasha Pervukhin is a DJ, producer, and member of the electronic duo Tysk, and a resident at Kharkiv's Center for New Culture Some People. Over 10+ years, he has explored styles from UK tech-house and deep house to breakbeat and raw techno, releasing music on labels such as Carpet & Snares, Rawax, Tesellate, and his own imprints Laconica and Pixel. He has performed at festivals like Brave Factory and СХЕМА, as well as clubs in Paris, Lisbon, and Berlin.
