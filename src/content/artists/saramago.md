Saramago is a Portuguese DJ and producer rooted in the formative nights of Lisbon's X-Club and Kaos Lisboa, spaces that helped shape his sound and musical identity. With years behind the decks and vinyl releases to his name, he continues to refine a dancefloor-focused approach driven by storytelling and groove. His recent work includes a new record on one of Portugal's most respected labels, reinforcing his presence in the country's underground electronic scene.
