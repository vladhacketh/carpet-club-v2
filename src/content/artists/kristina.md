Kristina is a Ukrainian-born DJ based in Lisbon and an active member of the Carpet & Snares collective. Her sets blend minimal, house, and dub techno with an intuitive and detail-driven approach shaped by deep digging and careful curation. A regular at Carpet parties and Lisbon's underground circuit, Kristina has performed at venues such as Ministerium Club, Harbour, and Rumu, continuing to build her presence through refined selections, vinyl discoveries, and a strong passion for electronic music.
