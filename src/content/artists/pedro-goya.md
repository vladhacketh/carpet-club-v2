Pedro Goya is a Portuguese electronic musician, DJ, and audio engineer known for his precise, dancefloor-focused sound and long-standing role in the underground scene. He has released music on respected labels such as Yoyaku, Bloop, Music For Freaks, and Carpet & Snares Records, alongside running his own imprint, Pedro Goya Music. Beyond production, he is also recognized for his mixing and mastering work and has performed internationally at venues including Watergate and Café d'Anvers.
