Bernardo Vaz began his career in 2011 under the alias Bill Onair, evolving from hip-hop and old-school R&B into a wide-ranging electronic music selector. With a deep passion for vinyl collecting, his sets move fluidly through disco, soul, house, techno, acid, trance, and electro. A resident at Harbour, Bernardo has performed at some of Lisbon's most respected venues, including Lux Frágil, Ministerium Club, and Galeria Zé dos Bois, while also contributing regularly to Collect's AUDIO TRIP radio show.
